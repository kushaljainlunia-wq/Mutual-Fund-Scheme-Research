🔍 Indian Mutual Fund Scheme Research

An institutional-grade, interactive HTML research report for analyzing any Indian mutual fund scheme. Provide a scheme name (e.g., "Parag Parikh Flexi Cap - Direct Growth") to get performance analysis, cost comparison, better alternatives in the same category, buy/sell recommendations, tax impact, and a scheme score out of 10.

About This Project

Welcome to my Indian Mutual Fund Scheme Research terminal, a project developed to perform structured and comprehensive analysis of individual Indian mutual fund schemes through live web data verification.


The research report integrates scheme-wise performance analysis (XIRR, returns, benchmark/category comparison), cost comparison (TER vs peers), better alternatives in the same category, action recommendations (Buy/Don't Buy/Wait), tax optimization opportunities, manager track record, AMC strength, and a 3-tier scoring framework into an interactive analytical framework.


The objective of this project is to transform scheme data into meaningful investment insights and establish a standardized approach for evaluating individual mutual fund schemes before investing or swapping.


Through this initiative, I aim to demonstrate the application of financial analysis, mutual fund expertise, data visualization, and analytical problem-solving in the field of mutual fund scheme research and investment decision-making.

From Data to Decisions

Mutual fund platforms and portfolio screeners excel at providing data. However, investment decisions require much more than numbers alone. Investors must understand fund quality, manager execution, cost efficiency, peer comparison, tax impact, and future growth prospects before building conviction.

This research terminal is designed as a research layer on top of mutual fund platforms. Rather than replacing portfolio screeners, it helps investors transform information into structured investment insights for a specific scheme.

🔍 Recommended Workflow

The most effective approach is to use this research terminal alongside mutual fund platforms and portfolio screeners.

  1. Use mutual fund platforms to discover and shortlist potential scheme opportunities.
  2. Provide the scheme name to the research terminal (e.g., "Parag Parikh Flexi Cap - Direct Growth").
  3. Understand fund quality, cost efficiency, manager execution, peer comparison, and tax impact.
  4. Review top 3 better alternatives in the same category (if available).
  5. Review action recommendations (Buy/Don't Buy/Wait/Swap) and tax considerations.
  6. Use the consolidated insights and scheme score to support investment decisions.

Key Philosophy

Mutual fund scheme data tells the story of the past. Investment decisions depend on understanding the future. This research terminal attempts to bridge that gap by combining fund analysis, performance review, benchmark comparison, cost analysis, risk evaluation, manager track record, AMC strength, peer comparison, tax reasoning, and buy/sell recommendations into a single research framework.
