🗣️ Plain-English Summary

UTI Silver ETF FoF is simply a wrapper that buys units of UTI's own Silver ETF, so your money tracks the domestic price of silver almost 1-for-1, minus a small fee. It doesn't pick stocks or make active calls — it's pure silver-price exposure, packaged as a regular mutual fund (no demat account needed) so you can start a SIP from ₹100. The fund itself is well-run and cheap for its category, but the real decision here isn't "is this a good fund" — it's "is this the right time and the right way to add silver," especially since you (via Mahendra's portfolio) already hold a Gold & Silver FoF.
